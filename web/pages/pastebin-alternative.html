When Pawste is the better fit

  • You're sharing something private, like config files, keys you've hidden, notes or a password, and don't want the site itself to be able to read it.
  • You want no ads and no account, on a page that looks good on a phone.
  • You want to update a paste and let readers see exactly what changed.

When another site may suit you better

  • PrivateBin if you want to run a paste service on your own server.
  • Pastebin if you want your public pastes listed and found by others on a big paste archive.

Good to know

Is Pawste a Pastebin alternative?

Yes. Like Pastebin you paste text or code and get a link. Unlike Pastebin, private links are encrypted in your browser and there are no ads.

Is Pawste free?

Yes, with no account. It's part of the free GmCat Toolbox.
