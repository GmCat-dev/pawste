      Good to know

      Is the note really deleted after it's read?

      Yes. When the reader presses Open, Pawste hands over the locked note and deletes it in the same step. Opening the link again shows “This paste is gone”.

      Can a chat app's link preview open it by accident?

      No. Apps like WhatsApp, Slack or iMessage only fetch the page, not the note. The note is only handed over when a person presses “Open it now”.

      Can GmCat read my note?

      No. The note is encrypted (AES-256) in your browser, and the key is the part of the link after “#”, which browsers never send to our server. We only store scrambled text, and delete even that once it's read.

      Should I add a password?

      If the link might be seen by someone else (for example in a shared chat), yes. Send the password another way, like a text message. The reader needs both.

      What if they never open it?

      It's deleted when the time you chose runs out (1 day unless you pick another). You can also delete it yourself from “Your pastes” on this device.

      Does it cost anything or need an account?

      No. It's free, with no account and no ads.
