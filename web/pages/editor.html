      Most paste sites show ads, track you and can read everything you post. Pawste has no ads, no account and no tracking, and it's private by default: a private link can't be read by GmCat even if it wanted to.

      Good to know

      Can GmCat read my pastes?

      Not private ones. A private paste is encrypted (AES-256) in your browser. The key is the part of the link after “#”, and browsers never send that part to a server, so we only ever store scrambled text.

      What's the difference between a private and a short link?

      A private link carries its own key, so it's long, and nobody without the link can read the paste, not even GmCat. A short link like paw.gmcat.io/k7m2x9pq is easy to share but isn't encrypted, so GmCat could read it; use it for code and notes, never for secrets. Short link plus a password is locked with the password instead: GmCat can't read it, but pick a strong password, because a weak one can be guessed.

      What if I lose the link?

      For a private paste, nobody can open it any more, including us, because we never have the key. Pastes you make are listed under “Your pastes” in the browser you made them in.

      What does Leak Guard check?

      Before you share, it looks for things like passwords, API keys and tokens, private keys, card numbers and recovery phrases. It runs on your device and never sends anything. It can miss things, so give your paste a quick look too.

      How long are pastes kept?

      You choose: from 10 minutes up to 1 year. Burn-after-reading pastes are deleted the first time they're opened. Each paste can be up to 512 KB of text.

      Can I change a paste after sharing it?

      Yes, with your edit link. The link stays the same, and readers can open the history to see each version and what changed. The first version and the 49 newest are kept.

      What isn't allowed?

      Anything illegal, harmful or hateful, malware, other people's private information, and spam. Readers can report a paste; after several reports it's hidden until we review it.
